Three findings, consistent across every codebase and cross-checked by the definition-plane probes (pretraining + source-conditioned regions, both falsified for 400x): (1) cold-strict single-build compressibility is an entropy floor set by the codebase — header/template-heavy libraries hit hundreds–thousands×, own-code-heavy engines cap near 150–270× — and no measured method (LZ z3..z19, LDM, front-code, skeleton-columnar, PPM/MTF, cross-project pretraining, trained zstd dict, source-conditioned COPY-programs) lifts the capped ones to 400×. (2) The S0 semantic-Root memo crushes 400× on the repeated-build basis (the production reality: a persistent per-daemon dictionary reused across builds) on every codebase, 3706–41102×. (3) Throughput is airtight: a real two-process socketpair sustains ≥1 GB/s single-stream, byte-exact, with linear multi-stream scaling.

2   Cold-strict FinalRatio — the entropy floor is a codebase property

Log scale. Same-scale codebases differ >10× (Eigen vs DuckDB at ~650 TUs each): the spread is the codebase, not the corpus size. The 400× line is unreachable cold for the own-code-heavy set by any method.

3   S0 amortized (40 builds) — ≥400x everywhere

Repeated-build basis: build 0 ships the dictionary cold; unchanged TUs in later builds are a 56-byte ROOT_REF. Every codebase clears 400× by 9–100×.

4   Why cold can't be fixed: coverage is inverse to dict size

The mechanism behind the cold floor, from defcodec's 16-codebase leave-one-out study (each corpus vs a prior built from the others). Red = big-dict corpora (≥3 MiB) — the ones whose one-time dictionary is the cold cost. They get the lowest cross-project coverage (LLVM/DuckDB ~5%, OpenCV 7%) because their own code dominates, so a pretrained/toolchain prior can't shrink them. The small-dict header-libs (spdlog/re2/cereal ~75%) are highly covered — pretraining helps them, but their dicts are already sub-MiB and irrelevant. Net: pretraining helps exactly the codebases that don't need it and cannot help the big-dict corpora that do — which is why no method reaches cold-400x on the bottleneck set.

(Cold FinalRatio vs own-source fraction is confounded by TU count — e.g. Eigen is 73% own-source yet 2131x cold because its header-only templates are hugely self-repetitive within a build, and tiny libs like spdlog have few TUs to amortize their dict. Dict-size×coverage is the clean axis pair; per-corpus own-source fractions are in study15-attribution.tsv for the record.)
